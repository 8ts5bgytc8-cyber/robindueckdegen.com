Twisting Theory of Lie Algebras

This project builds on work from my first summer research under the supervision of Dr. Curtis Wendlandt.

My first goal is to cover twisting theory of Lie algebras and coalgebras, with some perspective from (co)homology. I then hope to cover the twisting theory of Lie bialgebras, and potentially further, with some coverage of interpretation and applications.
