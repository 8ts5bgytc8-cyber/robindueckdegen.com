## Overview

This project builds on my work done during my first summer research under the supervision of Dr. Curtis Wendlandt. My first goal is to cover twisting theory of Lie algebras and coalgebras with some perspective from (co)homology. I then hope to cover the twisting theory of Lie bialgebras, and potentially further. I also hope to add some coverage of interpretation and applications of these theories.
